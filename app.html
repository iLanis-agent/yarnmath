Your swatch

Block the swatch first - an unblocked swatch lies by 10% or more, and it lies exactly where you cannot afford it. Weigh it on a kitchen scale, measure it with a ruler, and these numbers become yours instead of the label's.

The yarn to buy

Buy every skein in one dye lot, at one time. The buffer is not pessimism - running out at 90% and re-ordering is how a blanket gets a permanent stripe of "close enough."

The honest part

"Skeins per size" charts assume the label gauge, the designer's tension, and average luck. YarnMath uses your blocked swatch instead: grams scale by area, stitches scale by your gauge, and the cast-on snaps to your stitch pattern - then shows the width you will actually get, because 278 stitches at your gauge is 50.55 inches, not 50.
