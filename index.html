YarnMath

Honest yarn math: the swatch decides, not the label.

Cast on right

Your blocked swatch gives your real gauge, the target size gives the stitch count, and the stitch pattern snaps it to a legal multiple. YarnMath shows the width you will actually get - 278 stitches at your gauge is 50.55 inches, not 50 - so the surprise happens on screen, not after 40,000 stitches.

Buy enough

Weigh the swatch: grams scale by area, honestly. Project area over swatch area, times swatch weight, plus a buffer, rounded up to whole skeins - with the dye-lot warning front and center, because re-ordering mid-project is how blankets get stripes nobody planned.

Skip the charts

"Skeins per size" charts assume label gauge, designer tension, and average luck. Your hands are none of those. One weighed, blocked swatch replaces the whole chart.
